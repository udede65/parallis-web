PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

1. Overview & Data Controller

Welcome to Parallis: AI Dream Simulator ("Parallis", "we", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Apple App Store Guidelines, GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection laws. This Privacy Policy details how data is collected, processed, and secured when you use our mobile application and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only necessary information required to deliver high-quality AI lifestyle simulation features:

  • Profile Data: Simulated names, professions, cities, and dream goals created inside your parallel identity profiles.
  • AI Visual Inputs & Photos: Optional user reference photos provided for identity mapping. Photos are processed strictly for real-time visual generation via fal.ai (Nano Banana 2 Lite) model endpoints and are never sold or repurposed.
  • In-App Purchases & Analytics: Subscription transaction status, credit balances, and entitlement tokens managed via RevenueCat. We do not store raw credit card or financial numbers.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Non-personally identifiable ad identifiers (IDFA/GAID) handled via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for free-tier rewarded and interstitial ads.

3. How We Use Your Data

Data is processed exclusively to:

  • Generate customized luxury dream scene visuals, story scripts, and chronicle posts in your chosen language.
  • Maintain your VIP Subscription entitlements and simulation credit balances.
  • Provide customer support and process data deletion requests.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with industry-standard, secure infrastructure providers:

  • fal.ai (Nano Banana 2 Lite): High-performance AI visual generation engine.
  • RevenueCat: In-app purchase verification and entitlement management.
  • Google AdMob: Privacy-compliant ad delivery for non-VIP users.
  • Supabase: Encrypted cloud storage for user profiles and simulation history.

5. Data Retention & 1-Click Account Deletion

You maintain 100% ownership over your data. In full compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), you can permanently purge all your simulation profiles, history, and generated assets at any time:

  • Inside the App: Go to Profile Settings -> Tap "Delete Account" -> Confirm. Your account and all cloud data will be immediately and irreversibly deleted.
  • Online: Visit our Delete Account Page to submit a manual deletion request.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at parallisapps@gmail.com or via our Support Portal.
