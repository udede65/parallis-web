What is Parallis VIP Subscription?
Parallis VIP unlocks unlimited simulation profiles, ad-free experience, priority AI rendering, and exclusive luxury story text packs. Subscriptions auto-renew weekly or monthly and can be cancelled anytime via your Apple ID Settings.
How do Simulation Credit Packs work?
Credit packs (10, 25, 60, or 150 credits) are consumable top-ups that never expire. You can use them anytime to produce extra high-resolution AI vision scenes.

You can delete your account and all associated profiles instantly in the app under Profile -> Delete Account, or submit a request on our Delete Account web page.
